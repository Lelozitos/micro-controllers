Timing lines

Each line is one RC522 reader. The finish line counts laps. Sector lines go around the track in order and split each lap into sectors.

The Pi's SPI0 bus has two chip-select pins, CE0 (pin 24) and CE1 (pin 26), so /dev/spidev0.0 and /dev/spidev0.1 always exist, whether or not a reader is wired to them. Only the readers listed here are used.
